Privacy Policy

Effective 23 July 2026 · Last updated 24 July 2026

The short version: Broadsheet has no accounts and no servers of ours. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. Your settings and saved articles live only on your device. Data leaves your device only for the third-party services described below: advertising and app-store billing, plus the translation and market-price services the app calls when you use those features. None of those requests carry an account or an identifier, because there is none. On iOS, ads are only personalised if you say yes.

On this page

Who we are · What we collect · Data on your device · Third-party services · Advertising & your choices · Legal bases · Retention · Children · Your rights · Changes · Contact

1. Who we are

Broadsheet ("Broadsheet", "the app", "we", "us") is a mobile financial-news reader for Android and iOS, published by AgileDev. Broadsheet aggregates publicly available RSS/Atom feeds from news publishers and links you to their articles. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

You can reach us any time at support@agiledev.pro.

2. What we collect

We do not collect personal information. Broadsheet has no sign-up, no login, no email capture, and no user profiles. We do not ask for your name, address, phone number, or any identifier, and we operate no back-end servers that receive your data.

Because there is no account, there is nothing for us to lose, sell, or hand over. The information that does exist falls into two buckets: data that stays on your device (Section 3), and data handled by the third-party services the app calls — advertising, app-store billing, translation and market prices (Section 4).

3. Data stored on your device

To make the app useful, Broadsheet stores the following locally on your device only, using your device's standard app storage:

  • Your selected countries/markets and followed outlets;
  • Any custom sources you add (Premium), stored as the URLs you enter;
  • Your ticker/keyword watches, your instrument watchlist, and saved (bookmarked) articles;
  • App preferences such as theme, translation language and notification settings;
  • A rolling four-week archive of feed headlines, so the app works offline and can look back;
  • A cache of translations you have already requested, so the same text is never sent twice;
  • Local counters used to pace ads and show the paywall (e.g. how many articles you have opened).

This data never leaves your device through us. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, it is permanently gone. We cannot recover it, because we never had a copy.

4. Third-party services

Broadsheet relies on a small number of third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing the data it processes:

a) Google AdMob (advertising — free tier only)

Free-tier users see ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, AdMob may process a mobile advertising identifier, coarse location inferred from your IP address, device and diagnostic information, and ad-interaction events. Premium subscribers see no ads and this SDK is not used to serve them. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the AdMob privacy information.

b) App-store billing (subscriptions)

Premium subscriptions are purchased and managed entirely through Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple's In-App Purchase (iOS). Your payment details are handled by Google or Apple — Broadsheet never sees or stores your card, billing address, or store account. The app only receives a yes/no signal about whether an active subscription exists. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

c) News publishers & their feeds

Broadsheet fetches RSS/Atom feeds directly from publishers and, when you open a story, loads the publisher's own web page inside the app. At that point you are interacting with that publisher's site, and their privacy policy and cookies apply — exactly as if you had opened the link in a browser. We do not control and are not responsible for third-party sites.

d) Translation

When you translate a headline or an article, the text to be translated is sent to Google's public translation endpoint so it can be translated. That request carries the text and your device's IP address; it carries no account, no advertising identifier, and nothing identifying you, because the app holds none. Translations are cached on your device so the same text is only ever sent once, and nothing is sent at all unless you turn translation on. See the Google Privacy Policy.

e) Market prices

The Markets tab and the price chips on stories request quotes and charts from Yahoo Finance's public endpoints, and cryptocurrency prices from CoinGecko. Those requests contain the symbols being displayed or searched and your device's IP address — again, no account and no identifier. Nothing about your reading, watchlist, or saved articles is sent to them. See the Yahoo Privacy Policy and the CoinGecko Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising and your choices

  • iOS — App Tracking Transparency: On iOS, Broadsheet asks for your permission before allowing personalised ads. If you decline, you still get the full app; the ads are simply less relevant.
  • Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Remove ads entirely: A Premium subscription turns ads off completely.

6. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, our processing rests on: your consent for personalised advertising (which you can withhold or withdraw as described above); and our and our partners' legitimate interests in showing non-personalised ads and preventing fraud, and in operating the app you asked to use. We are not the controller for the payment data handled by Google or Apple.

7. Data retention

On-device data is retained until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Data processed by AdMob, Google, and Apple is retained according to their respective policies, not ours.

8. Children

Broadsheet is a financial-news product intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through a third-party service in connection with the app, contact us and we will help you address it with the relevant provider.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data and to object to certain processing. Because Broadsheet holds no personal data about you on any server, the most direct way to exercise these rights over app data is to clear the app's storage or uninstall it. For data held by AdMob, Google, or Apple, please use their tools and contact them directly; we are happy to help point you to the right place — email support@agiledev.pro.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves or the law changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and, where appropriate, noted in the app. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email support@agiledev.pro and we'll respond as quickly as we can.
