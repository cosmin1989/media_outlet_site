Why Broadsheet

An aggregator that reads like a newspaper, not a terminal.

Publisher apps are single-source and paywalled. RSS readers are powerful and empty. Broadsheet is the finance-native middle: a pre-vetted catalog, a country lens, and your own sources.

The country lens

Choose the markets you actually follow. Broadsheet blends their financial press into one chronological feed — a view no store competitor offers.

Add any source on earth

Paste any site or feed URL — your local business paper, a niche markets blog, an analyst's feed. Broadsheet auto-discovers the feed and previews it before you add it.

Premium

No account. No tracking wall.

No sign-up, no email, no profile. Your markets, outlets and saved articles live on your device — not on our servers, because there are none.

Ticker & keyword watches

Follow a ticker, a company or a theme and get a real-time alert the moment it surfaces across your outlets — matched on your device, with no server watching over your shoulder.

Five editorial themes

Ink and Newsprint free; Carbon (OLED), Slate and Parchment for members. Serif headlines, hairline rules — designed to read like the press it aggregates.

Always read at the source

Stories open the publisher's own page inside the app — their layout, their ads, their byline. Broadsheet never rehosts an article, so the journalism and the revenue stay with the newsroom.

Live markets, not just headlines

Prices, charts and index levels for 122 countries, plus universal search across every exchange and asset class. Star what you hold and its news floats to the top of your feed.

Track what you hold

Attach your shares and cost to any starred instrument and Broadsheet shows live value, today's move and total P&L — the same on-device prices, no brokerage login, nothing leaves the phone.

Premium

Read Handelsblatt in your language

Headlines and whole articles translate into any of 17 languages, in place on the publisher's own page. A 125-country catalogue is only useful if you can read it.

Topics, and no story twice

Filter to Policy, Banking, Energy or Crypto. When ten outlets cover the same story you get one card that names the rest — a feed that stays readable as you add markets.

How it works

Useful ten seconds after you install.

STEP 01

Pick your markets

Tap the countries you follow. Broadsheet ships with a pre-vetted catalog of 248 financial outlets — no RSS literacy, no empty inbox to fill.

STEP 02

Scan one clean feed

Every outlet in your markets, merged and sorted by time. Kicker, serif headline, snippet — built to scan, not to shout.

STEP 03

Read it without leaving

The publisher's own page opens inside the app — translated into your language if you want it. Save it, set a watch, or add your own sources when one feed isn't enough.

Questions

The honest answers

Does Broadsheet republish articles?

No. Broadsheet shows a headline, a short snippet and the publisher's name, then links you straight to the original article on the publisher's own site. The journalism and the ad revenue stay with the newsroom — it's an index, not a copy.

Do I need an account?

Never. There's no sign-up, no email and no profile. Your chosen markets, followed outlets and saved articles are stored locally on your device. We run no servers that hold your data, because we run no servers at all.

What does "add my own sources" mean?

With Premium you can paste any website or feed URL — a local business paper, a niche markets blog, an analyst's newsletter — and Broadsheet auto-discovers its feed and folds it into your stream. If a site publishes a feed, you can read it here.

How much does it cost?

The app is free, including the full 248-outlet catalog and one country lens. Premium is $6.99 / month or $50.99 / year. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled, and you can cancel anytime from your app store account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Broadsheet is offline-first: your feed and saved articles are cached on the device, so the app opens instantly and your reading list is there even without a connection.

Which platforms is it on?

Broadsheet is built for both Android and iOS and is currently in store review. Join by grabbing it from Google Play or the App Store the moment it's live.
